**Screen:** Shopify store page

**Me:** Hi, When an online order comes in, many small businesses have to manually enter sales into Tally, update spreadsheets, send gst invoice to customers.

That's repetitive work that costs time and requires hiring people just to move data between systems.

I wanted to see how much of this could be automated, So I built an end-to-end workflow for an online store.

To show you how this works, I created a demo store on Shopify.

Let's say I wanna buy this snowboard, I add them to cart, fillup the details and click "Pay now".

**Screen:** Shopify admin page

As you can see in the admin panel, a new order is created worth (the amount).

**Screen:** TallyPrime home

**Me:** Now if the customer ledger doesn't already exist in Tally, it creates the ledger first, otherwise it directly creates the sales voucher.

**Screen:** Navigating to Sales Voucher screen

**Me:** You can see the voucher, with calculated gst splits and the total amount.

**Screen:** Gmail.

**Me:** Next, the customer gets an order confirmation email within a few seconds, with the tax invoice attached.

**Screen:** Opening the invoice pdf.

**Me:** There you go. The invoice is generated with seller info, customer info, line items, their gst splits and the total payable.

**Screen:** Shopify admin page

**Me:** On the other hand, the order is marked "invoiced" in Shopify admin panel.

**Screen:** Google sheet

**Me:** And the order details are maintained in seller's internal spreadsheet, for future analytics.

**Screen:** Telegram.

**Me:** Finally when all these processes are complete or if there is any failure, the seller gets real-time notification informing them the order status.

This took a few seconds—no manual data entry, no more duplicate bookkeeping.
